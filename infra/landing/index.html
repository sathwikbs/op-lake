How access works

  • One identity: Keycloak is the IdP; every tool uses OIDC SSO against the same realm.
  • One data authority: Unity Catalog resolves your token to a principal and enforces grants — the same rules whether you use Superset, Jupyter, Dagster or local dbt.
  • Personas & groups: membership of a Keycloak group grants a persona (analyst, data-engineer, platform-admin); a reconciler materializes it as per-principal UC grants and app-level roles.
  • Automation vs humans: Dagster runs pipelines as team service accounts (passwordless); people run as themselves. No shared admin behind anything.
  • Local dev: eval "$(bin/uc-login)" then run dbt or a notebook on your laptop as yourself.
